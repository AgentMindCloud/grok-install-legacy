Frequently Asked Questions

Will my agent spam my followers?
No. It only replies to mentions that tag you. It never posts on its own or DMs anyone.
Can I delete my agent later?
Yes — instantly. One click and it’s gone forever. No residual permissions.
How much does it cost?
You set the daily cap (default $3). Most users spend $0.10–$0.80/day. You only pay for what your agent actually uses.
What permissions does Sign in with X request?
Only read access to your public profile and recent posts. Never write or DM access.
Can the agent hallucinate or say something harmful?
Every reply is pre-screened by Grok’s safety layer (99%+ pass rate). You can also add custom guardrails.
What is Voice DNA?
We read your last 200 public posts and turn your unique writing style into a system prompt. It’s a digital clone of how you sound on X.
